Engine

How the score works. Each component is computed only from Unusual Whales data and returns a signed direction × strength. Net = weighted average of available directional components. Signal Score = |Net| × data-coverage × 100 — high scores need multiple real signals to agree and enough data present. It is a ranking/confidence score, not a probability of profit, and nothing here is financial advice. Prediction-market analogs (Outcome Pressure ≈ PCR, Liquidity/Depth ≈ OI) are labelled honestly; no option chain is invented.
